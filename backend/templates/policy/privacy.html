Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

DonateBox ("we", "us", or "the app") is a personal donation application operated by {{ recipient }}. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a donation through DonateBox, we may collect:

  • Name — only if you choose to provide it; donations can be made anonymously.
  • Donation amount and currency — to process your payment.
  • Payment reference IDs — transaction identifiers from the payment gateway (Razorpay or Stripe). We do not store your card number, UPI PIN, bank account details, or any other payment credentials.
  • Device and IP metadata — standard request headers and IP addresses for rate limiting and security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and verify your donation payment.
  • To display donation records in our internal admin dashboard (accessible only to the app administrator).
  • To comply with applicable financial regulations and tax requirements.
  • To protect against abuse, fraud, and security threats.

3. Payment Processing

Payments are processed securely by third-party gateways: Razorpay (for INR donations) and Stripe (for USD donations). Your payment credentials are handled entirely by these providers — they never pass through or are stored on our servers.

We never see, store, or have access to your card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN, or bank login credentials. All payment data is handled directly by the payment gateway's secure, PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

4. Data Retention

Donation records (name if provided, amount, status, and payment reference) are retained indefinitely for accounting and compliance purposes. You may request deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties, except:

  • Payment gateways (Razorpay / Stripe) for processing your transaction.
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including HTTPS encryption for all communications, server-side payment verification, webhook signature validation, rate limiting, and secure admin authentication.

7. Your Rights

You may:

  • Request access to your donation records.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Make donations anonymously without providing a name.
