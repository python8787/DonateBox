Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

DonateBox is a personal donation application operated by {{ recipient }}. Because donations are voluntary gifts and not purchases, our refund policy differs from a typical e-commerce return policy.

1. General Policy

Donations are generally non-refundable. By completing a donation, you acknowledge that you are making a voluntary gift and do not expect goods or services in return.

2. When Refunds May Be Issued

We may issue a refund in the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate charges — if you were charged more than once for the same donation due to a technical error.
  • Incorrect amount — if the amount charged differs significantly from what you intended due to a system error.
  • Unauthorized transaction — if you believe a donation was made without your authorization.
  • Payment failure with charge — if your payment was marked as failed but the amount was deducted from your account.

3. Refund Process

To request a refund:

  • Contact us within 30 days of the transaction date.
  • Provide your donation details (amount, date, and any transaction reference you have).
  • We will review your request and respond within 5 business days.

Approved refunds are processed through the original payment method (Razorpay or Stripe). The refund timeline depends on the payment gateway and your bank — typically 5–10 business days.

4. Cancellation

DonateBox does not offer recurring subscriptions. Each donation is a one-time transaction. If you initiated a payment but have not completed it, you can simply close the payment window — no charge will be made.

If a payment is in "pending" status for an extended period, it will eventually expire based on the payment gateway's timeout policy. No action is needed from you.

5. Disputes

If you dispute a charge with your bank or card issuer, please also contact us so we can help resolve the issue directly. Chargebacks incur fees from payment gateways, and we prefer to resolve issues amicably.
