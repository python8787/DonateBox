# DonateBox — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

## What We Collect

- **Donor name** (optional — you may donate anonymously)
- **Donation amount and currency**
- **Payment reference ID** (from the payment gateway)
- **Timestamp** of the donation

## What We Do NOT Collect or Store

- Credit/debit card numbers
- CVV/CVC codes
- UPI PIN
- Bank account details
- Net banking credentials

All payment processing is handled securely by our payment partners (Razorpay for INR, Stripe for USD). We never see or store your payment credentials.

## How We Use Your Information

- To process and confirm your donation
- To provide donation receipts
- To maintain records for reconciliation
- To respond to your inquiries

## Data Retention

Donation records are retained for accounting and legal compliance purposes. You may request deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

## Third-Party Services

- **Razorpay** (INR payments) — [Razorpay Privacy Policy](https://razorpay.com/privacy/)
- **Stripe** (USD payments) — [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy)
